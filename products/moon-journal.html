Welcome

The moon has always been a mirror — reflecting the tides within us, the cycles we live through, the emotions we carry. This journal is an invitation to pay attention.

Over 28 days, you will move through one complete lunar cycle. Each day has a prompt aligned with the moon's energy, a space for gratitude, and a mood check-in. There are no rules. This is your space.

You will also find angel number guidance woven through each day — gentle reminders from the universe that you are always supported, always seen, always loved.

New Moon

Days 1–7. Plant seeds. Set intentions. What do you want to call in? This is the time of beginnings and quiet magic.

Waxing Moon

Days 8–14. Build momentum. Take action. What steps are you taking toward your dreams? Trust the growth.

Full Moon

Days 15–21. Illuminate. Release. What no longer serves you? Celebrate how far you've come.

Waning Moon

Days 22–28. Rest. Reflect. Let go. Prepare for the new cycle. You are always in a cycle — trust it.
