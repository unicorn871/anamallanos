The woman behind Alana

Hi, I'm Ana

I'm a lawyer, traveler, and life enthusiast who believes that the most important journey is the one inward. I created Alana as a space for women who want to live with more intention — to know themselves better, love deeper, and design a life that actually feels like theirs.

I guide women through clarity sessions and intuitive conversations — blending deep dialogue with tarot as a tool for self-reflection — to help them find direction, make aligned decisions, and step into the life they truly want.

— Ana ✦
